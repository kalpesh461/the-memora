Our story

We started because a good gift is hard to find

The Memora exists for the fifteen minutes between someone picking up a box and putting it down again — and everything that stays after.

Why we exist

The gift was fine. The giving was not.

The Memora began with a birthday gift that arrived in a courier bag, with the price sticker still on the box and an invoice tucked inside. The jewellery was lovely. The moment was ruined.

So we built the opposite. Every Memora order is packed as if it's being handed over in person — because it usually is. Rigid lined boxes, hand-tied satin, a wax seal, a written card, and never, ever a price tag inside.

We are a small studio in India. We answer our own WhatsApp, we photograph every order before it ships, and we would rather turn down a rush order than send something we're not proud of.

What we believe

Four rules we don't break

01

No price inside

Never an invoice, never a sticker. What it cost is your business.

02

Packed by a person

Hands, not machines. Every ribbon is tied and every seal is pressed.

03

It has to last

Anti-tarnish plating and real materials. A gift that fades isn't a keepsake.

04

We reply fast

WhatsApp in minutes, not "within 48 business hours".
